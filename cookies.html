1. What Are Cookies?

Definition: Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies typically contain:

  • The name of the website they come from
  • How long they will stay on your device
  • A unique identifier (usually a random string of numbers and letters)

Cookies are not programs and cannot contain viruses or malware. They cannot access information stored on your computer.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

Essential Operation

To keep you logged in, maintain your session, and ensure website security.

Preferences

To remember your settings like language, currency, and layout preferences.

Analytics

To understand how visitors use our website and improve its performance.

Marketing

To deliver relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Purpose: These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website.

Consent: These cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for the website to work. They do not store any personally identifiable information.

Examples: Session cookies, authentication cookies, security cookies.

Functional Cookies

Purpose: These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features.

Consent: We ask for your consent to place these cookies on your device.

Examples: Language preference cookies, region selection cookies.

Analytics Cookies

Purpose: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

Consent: We ask for your consent to place these cookies on your device.

Examples: Google Analytics cookies that track page views, time on site, and referral sources.

Marketing Cookies

Purpose: These cookies are used to track visitors across websites. The intention is to display ads that are relevant and engaging for individual users.

Consent: We ask for your explicit consent to place these cookies on your device.

Examples: Facebook Pixel, Google Ads conversion tracking.

5. How to Manage Cookies

5.1 Using Our Cookie Consent Tool

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Manage Cookie Preferences" button at the top of this page or by clicking the cookie icon in the bottom corner of our website.

Quick Preferences

You can choose to:

  • Accept All: Allow all categories of cookies
  • Essential Only: Only allow essential cookies
  • Customize: Choose exactly which categories you want to allow
  • Withdraw Consent: Change your mind at any time

5.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, limiting cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Browser Cookie Management Guide

Google Chrome
  1. Click the three dots in the top right
  2. Go to Settings > Privacy and security
  3. Click "Cookies and other site data"
  4. Choose your preferred settings
Mozilla Firefox
  1. Click the menu button and select Settings
  2. Go to Privacy & Security
  3. Under "Cookies and Site Data", choose your settings
Safari
  1. Go to Safari > Preferences
  2. Click the Privacy tab
  3. Under "Cookies and website data", choose your settings
Microsoft Edge
  1. Click the three dots and select Settings
  2. Go to Cookies and site permissions
  3. Click "Cookies and site data"
  4. Adjust your settings

5.3 Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites you do not want to be tracked. Currently, we do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals because there is no industry standard for how to respond.
