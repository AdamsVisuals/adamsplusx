1. Introduction

Welcome to Adams+ Digital Solutions ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact us at hello@adamsplusx.com.

Scope: This privacy policy applies to all information collected through our website https://adamsplusx.com ("Website"), and/or any related services, sales, marketing, or events (we refer to them collectively in this privacy policy as the "Services").

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We comply with applicable data protection laws, including:

  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA)
  • The Personal Data Protection Act of Tanzania
  • Other applicable data protection laws based on your location

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide to Us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out contact forms or project inquiry forms
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Request information about our services
  • Apply for a job with us
  • Contact us via email or phone

The personal information we collect may include:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Project Details: Information about your project requirements, budget, timeline
  • Business Information: Company name, website, business type
  • Communication Data: Messages, emails, and other communications

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

When you visit our Website, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing behavior, including:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type and version, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns
  • Location Data: General location (city/country level) based on IP address
  • Technical Data: Device identifiers, crash reports, system activity
Note: We do not collect sensitive personal information (such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, or sexual orientation) unless voluntarily provided by you for specific purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various business purposes, including:

3.1 To Provide and Improve Our Services

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process project inquiries and prepare proposals
  • Develop and improve our website and services
  • Personalize your experience on our website

3.2 Communication

  • Send you important updates about our services
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent)
  • Send administrative information (changes to terms, policies, etc.)

3.3 Legal Compliance and Security

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and prevent fraud
  • Ensure the security of our website and services
  • Investigate and prevent security incidents

3.4 Analytics and Research

  • Analyze website usage and improve user experience
  • Conduct market research and analyze trends
  • Measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns
  • Develop new products and services

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR): For users in the EEA, we process your personal information based on:

  • Consent: When you have given us permission
  • Contract: To fulfill our contractual obligations
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with legal requirements
  • Legitimate Interests: For our legitimate business interests

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except in the following circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or serving our users, including:

  • Email Service Providers: For sending emails and newsletters
  • Hosting Providers: For website hosting and data storage
  • Analytics Providers: For website analytics and performance monitoring
  • Payment Processors: For processing payments (if applicable)

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information where required by law or if we believe such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations or legal processes
  • Protect and defend our rights or property
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with our Services
  • Protect the personal safety of users or the public

4.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on our website of any change in ownership or uses of your personal information.

Data Protection Agreements: All third-party service providers are required to maintain the confidentiality of your information and are prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing services to us.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, please also remember that we cannot guarantee that the internet itself is 100% secure.

5.1 Security Measures

Our security measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using SSL/TLS
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Regular data backups and disaster recovery plans
  • Employee training on data protection best practices

5.2 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Our retention periods are:

  • Inquiry Data: 3 years from last contact
  • Newsletter Subscribers: Until unsubscribed
  • Client Data: 7 years from project completion (for tax and legal purposes)
  • Website Analytics: 26 months

Your Responsibility: You are responsible for keeping your login credentials (if any) confidential. Please notify us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized access to your account or personal information.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

6.1 General Rights

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal information
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

6.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@adamsplusx.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

Verification: We may need to verify your identity before processing your request to ensure the security of your personal information.

6.3 Complaints

If you believe we have not addressed your concern satisfactorily, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For users in Tanzania, you may contact:

Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)
(Note: As of 2025, Tanzania is developing its data protection framework. We will update this information once the commission is officially established.)

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Website and store certain information.

7.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

7.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function
  • Performance Cookies: Collect information about how visitors use our website
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Track visitors across websites for advertising purposes

7.3 Cookie Management

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Important: If you disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly.

7.4 Third-Party Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic. Google Analytics collects information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Referring websites
  • Demographic information (age, gender, interests)

You can opt-out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

8. Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to other websites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

No Control: We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from children under 16 without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information, including personal information, may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction where the data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction.

If you are located outside Tanzania and choose to provide information to us, please note that we transfer the data to Tanzania and process it there.

Data Transfer Safeguards: We take all steps reasonably necessary to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this privacy policy. For transfers to countries not deemed to provide an adequate level of data protection, we implement appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

We will let you know via email and/or a prominent notice on our Website, prior to the change becoming effective and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this privacy policy.

You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this privacy policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
